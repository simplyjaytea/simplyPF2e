11. (Optional, zero spend) As a non-GM or with pf2e absent, confirm the tool is refused. Record only if tested; otherwise mark "not tested" in the paste-back.

Gate back off:

12. Disable **Enable experimental tools** again.

14. Confirm Hazard mode is absent and the final state is gate off.
