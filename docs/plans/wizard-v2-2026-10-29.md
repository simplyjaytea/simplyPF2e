**Status:** implemented on `work/wizard-v2` (not pushed, not merged). Native rows N16–N18 are OPEN. Backlog §9 item 3 in [PLAYBOOK](../../PLAYBOOK.md). Builds on [wave 2](sprint-2026-10-28-wave2.md) and [wizard research](wizard-research-2026-10-26.md).

## Part 1: advanced school spell

### pf2e evidence (`pf2e-8.5.0`, quoted from the raw files)

The feat is `packs/pf2e/feats/class/wizard/level-8/advanced-school-spell.json` (`_id` `5hUj7glY8YnO5sBI`). The same path exists at `v14-dev` (tree listing), so the id is stable across the two refs the module checks.

- line 5: `"name": "Advanced School Spell",`
- line 15: `"value": "<p>You gain access to the advanced teachings of your arcane institution, learning a powerful new school spell. You gain the advanced school spell for your arcane school.</p>"`
- line 18: `"value": 8` (level 8)
- line 23: `"value": "arcane school"` (a text prerequisite, not a ChoiceSet)
- line 32: `"rules": [],`; the file has no `items` key, so the feat grants nothing.

The school carries the advanced link in the same paragraph as the initial one, `packs/pf2e/class-features/school-of-battle-magic.json` line 14:

> `<p><strong>School Spells</strong> initial: @UUID[Compendium.pf2e.spells-srd.Item.Force Bolt]; advanced: @UUID[Compendium.pf2e.spells-srd.Item.Energy Absorption]</p>`

The school's own rules are `[]`, and the Arcane School bridge grants only the school the player chose (`packs/pf2e/class-features/arcane-school.json`: `ChoiceSet` then `GrantItem` of `{item|flags.system.rulesSelections.arcaneSchool}`).

### Grant decision: no grant, so the module adds the spell

pf2e grants neither the initial nor the advanced school spell through a rule. The module therefore embeds the advanced spell when the built PC has the Advanced School Spell feat and spellcasting is on. No duplicate is possible because pf2e adds nothing.

- The feat is detected by its document id (`ADVANCED_SCHOOL_SPELL_FEAT_ID`) in the built feat item's `_stats.compendiumSource`, or in a `{ packId, _id }` reference, so a feat with the same name from another source does not match.
- The advanced spell comes from the staged school's link, in name form or built id form, through the same parser as the initial spell (`schoolSpellLink`, with label `advanced`). It is a focus spell in an enabled pack, or it is skipped with a warning.
- It goes into the same focus entry as the initial spell, with `rules: []` on that entry. No Rule Element is hand-authored.
- The starting `resources.focus.value` counts the focus spells embedded (two here). pf2e derives the maximum from embedded non-cantrip focus spells; the module writes no focus-pool rule (PLAYBOOK §3, the 2026-10-28 double-count correction).

### Sibling feat not built

`packs/pf2e/feats/archetype/wizard/arcane-school-spell.json` (8.5.0, line 5 "Arcane School Spell", line 18 `"value": 4`, line 23 `"value": "Wizard Dedication"`, line 15 "You gain your school's initial school spell") is the dedication version. The module offers no Wizard dedication (the multiclass catalog is Fighter, Investigator, Barbarian, Monk and Ranger), so it is not built.

## Part 2: school spell in the PC preview

- **Fixed before Create:** the school is fixed when either the concept names it (`requiredIdentity.classPaths["Arcane School"]`, a candidate from `resolvePCIdentity`) or the class offers exactly one closed Arcane School candidate (`getClassPathCandidates`, the same list the chooser sees). The school document is loaded through the candidate's issued `ref` (`getCandidateDocument`). The preview then shows the initial spell and, when the Advanced School Spell feat was picked, the advanced one.
- **Chosen at Create:** the class has an Arcane School bridge but the school is not named. The preview shows `SIMPLYPF2E.Preview.SchoolSpellAtCreate` ("School spell: chosen with the school at Create").
- **No line:** no spellcasting, or no Arcane School bridge on the class.
- A spell that cannot be loaded shows as "not found in the school description". No name is guessed.
- The single-closed-candidate case is built and tested with a world that holds one school. With every 8.5.0 pack enabled the Wizard has 14 closed schools, so the case needs fewer schools in the enabled packs.
- Names reach the page through the Handlebars `{{this}}` escape, as invariant 5 requires.

## Part 3: uncommon cantrip decks

### pf2e evidence (`pf2e-8.5.0`)

- `src/module/item/consumable/spell-consumables.ts` lines 43–45: `const traits = consumableSource.system.traits;` / `traits.value = R.unique([...traits.value, ...spell.system.traits.value]);` / `traits.rarity = spell.rarity;`
- `src/scripts/config/spell-consumables.ts` lines 64–79: the `cantripDeck5` entry, a template UUID for rank 1 and `cantripsOnly: true` on line 79. It has no rarity field.

Neither file restricts the rarity of a cantrip deck. The deck takes the cantrip's rarity.

### Decisions

- Common and uncommon cantrips are offered. The Forge caps the deck pool at uncommon: a common Forge offers common cantrips only, and an uncommon, rare or unique Forge offers up to uncommon.
- The deck's rarity is the cantrip's rarity, copied as in pf2e line 45 (`buildSpellConsumable`).
- Rare and unique cantrips stay excluded. The rule is the module's own policy, not a pf2e rule: pf2e has no rule against them, and no rule allows them either. This is a judgment for the user to confirm.
- The rest of the gating is unchanged: rank 1, a cantrip only, no focus cantrip, no ritual, and the pinned template.
