# C09 — Free Archetype implementation

Branch `t3/c09`, based on C08 HEAD `ac4e954`. No push, no merge to main.

## Scope delivered

- **Level-2+ gate removed.** The former `freeArchetypeNeedsPrerequisiteValidation` preflight in `generator-app.mjs` and `pc-support.mjs` is deleted. Level-2+ Free Archetype characters now go through the normal one-click path.
- **Catalog-only archetype slots.** Each `{type:"class", archetype:true}` slot (emitted by `buildFeatSlots` at even class-feat levels when Free Archetype is on) draws only from `multiclassFeatCandidates`. Generic archetype feats are excluded. A pick outside the catalog is refused by `pickBlocker` in `pc-builder.mjs` with `not in the Free Archetype catalog`.
- **Catalog:** five dedications from the PF2e 8.5.0 source, with their package feats: Fighter, Investigator, Barbarian, Monk, Ranger Dedication.
- **Ability prerequisites.** Catalog packages carry `abilities: [{key, min}]`. `multiclassAbilityBlocker(name, abilities)` in `pc-multiclass.mjs` returns:
  - `null` for non-dedications or dedications with no requirement;
  - `<Name> needs ability modifiers that are not known` when the slot has no derived modifiers;
  - `<Name> needs <Ability> +<min>` when the derived modifier is too low.
- **Derived modifiers per slot.** `plannedAbilitySections` mirrors the ancestry/background/class/voluntary boost plan, and `deriveAbilityModifiers` applies it per slot level (+1, +0.5 at ≥4 for the fifth boost, −1 flaw, clamp to [−5, 10]). Unknown keys throw. These modifiers are not passed to `stagedActorContext`, because `clauseMet` does not understand `+N` and would fail closed.
- **Locks.** Same-class dedications are refused. Cross-slot dedication locks follow the multiclass lock already used by C08.
- **PC edit path.** `applyFeatEdit` in `pc-edit.mjs` rejects ability-blocked picks with `PC_EDIT_CODES.INVALID`. `remapFeats` invalidates ability-blocked picks.
- **Settings and copy.** `FreeArchetype.Hint` in `lang/en.json` and the comment in `settings.mjs` describe catalog-only slots with ability checks. `FreeArchetypeUnsupported` is removed.
